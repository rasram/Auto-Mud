About AutoMUD

Understand what you’re seeing

Device behavior, network permissions and response decisions are separate checks.

GNN signals

The GNN checks device behavior and connection patterns. A raised flag is a signal to investigate, not a confirmed compromise or a probability.

Network policy

MUD policies define permitted communication. A policy violation can occur even when the GNN raises no flag.

Review before response

Actions depend on evidence quality and the approved response policy. Monitoring mode records findings without automatically restricting traffic.

Technical details and release information

Raw scores use fixed model thresholds. Model releases, policies and source identifiers are retained here for troubleshooting.
